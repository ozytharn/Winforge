# WinForge Privacy

## Local-first design

WinForge is designed to keep evidence local and read-only. The app does not automatically upload telemetry or diagnostics.

## Export boundary

Any support package must intentionally filter out passwords, tokens, secrets, private files, and unrelated document content.

## Data retention

The local evidence database and settings are retained under the user profile. This supports evidence review and issue diagnosis without hidden remote transmission.
